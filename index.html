Part I: The Forge Optimization (Global Maxima & 2nd Derivative) Core Mechanic

A derivative tells you how damage changes when you add Strength. Split 10 points between Strength x and Focus (10 − x).

1. Damage: D(x) = 6x(10 − x) = 60x − 6x².
2. Differentiate term by term: 60x becomes 60; −6x² becomes −12x. So D′(x) = 60 − 12x.
3. Find the flat point: 60 − 12x = 0, so x = 5. Put 5 points in Strength and 5 in Focus.
4. Check the peak: D″(x) = −12. The curve bends downward. D(0) = D(10) = 0, so D(5) = 150 is the highest damage on [0, 10].
5. Try it: at x = 3, D = 126 and D′ = +24 (add Strength). At x = 7, D = 126 and D′ = −24 (add Focus). At x = 5, D′ = 0 and damage is 150.
Part II: Release halfway through the charge

Let T be the charge duration in seconds and u = t/T be the fraction of time elapsed. For a normal 4-second charge, halfway is 2 seconds.

1. Gauge fill: R(t) = 3(t/T)² − 2(t/T)³. It starts at 0 and ends at 1.
2. Fill speed: R′(t) = 6u(1 − u)/T. Acceleration: R″(t) = (6 − 12u)/T².
3. Set R″ = 0: 6 − 12u = 0, so u = 1/2. Before halfway the speed increases; after halfway it decreases. The center is the inflection point and the fastest fill.
4. Timing score = R′ / maximum R′ = 4u(1 − u). Damage multiplier = 0.5 + 1.5 × score. At halfway it is 2×; at either end it is 0.5×.
5. At Level 1 with a 5/5 build, a center hit deals 150 × 0.6 × 2 = 180 damage. An end hit deals 45. Your build and timing both matter!

Your first steps in Emberbrook
Explore and talk

Hold WASD or the arrow keys, or drag the joystick. Press E / Space or Action near someone to talk or fight. Walk through the marked doorways to travel.

Start with the village

Visit the Elder in the blue-roofed house on the left. The blacksmith in the red-roofed house heals you for free and sells potions.

Read the enemy. Time your strike. Protect the harvest.

Attack starts a charge: release at the center for a strong hit. Item opens your potions; each restores 40 HP and uses a turn. Run can escape forest monsters, but guardians block your way.

Bring the harvest home

Follow the north path into Whispering Forest. Reach Level 3 for the northwest cavern. Defeat its Golem, reach Level 5, then take the northeast path to Ember Summit.

If you fall

You return to the village with full health and lose half your gold. Your level and quest progress stay with you. After an encounter, Battle details shows how your attacks went.
